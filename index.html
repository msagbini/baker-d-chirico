Our Story

Founded by Daniel Chirico in Melbourne's Carlton in 2001, Baker D. Chirico has become an icon of Australian artisan baking.

Daniel’s journey began with a simple yet radical idea: a bakery that refuses to be defined by a single tradition. French-trained, Italian by heritage, and a lover of German breads, he created a space where sourdough, croissants and bomboloni coexist in perfect harmony.

Now with a flagship in Newstead, Brisbane, the bakery continues to push boundaries, blending old-world techniques with contemporary design and a touch of surrealism.

“I never wanted to have a bakery that was Italian only because I was French trained and I love German bread.” — Daniel Chirico
